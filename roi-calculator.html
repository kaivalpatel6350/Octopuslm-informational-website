How the estimate is calculated

  1. Manual hours = pages per month ÷ pages per hour.
  2. Manual cost = manual hours × hourly cost.
  3. Hours with OctopusLM = manual hours × verification %. You still open the cited pages that matter to the decision; you stop reading the ones that don't.
  4. OctopusLM cost = the cheaper of $0.10 × pages or $250 flat. The break-even is 2,500 pages a month.
  5. Net saving = reviewer time saved − OctopusLM cost.

What the calculator deliberately leaves out: faster reserving, fewer outsourced summary invoices, shorter cycle times, and the value of a chronology that survives a challenge because every line cites a page.

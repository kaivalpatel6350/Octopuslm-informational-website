How it works

Three steps from a stack of PDFs to a finished, cited chronology.

1. Upload

Drop in the PDFs exactly as you received them. Out of order, mixed providers, thousands of pages — no prep needed.

2. It reads every page

Diagnoses, treatments, providers, and dates are pulled out and put in chronological order automatically.

3. Review and export

Read the summary, click any citation to jump to the source page, edit what you want, then export to Word or Excel.

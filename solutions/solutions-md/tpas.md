## The TPA problem is consistency at volume

A TPA's medical review process has to work across clients with different programs, different jurisdictions, and different reporting expectations — while every adjuster on the floor produces a file that looks like it came from the same shop. Manual chronologies cannot do that at volume. Outsourced summaries can, but they add days and a per-file invoice to every claim.

## What OctopusLM gives a TPA

- **One workflow for every program.** Upload the medical PDFs from any client file; get the same structured summary and dated chronology back, with a page citation on every line.
- **Same-day turnaround** instead of a vendor queue. A file that arrives at 9 a.m. is summarized before the adjuster's first call.
- **Multi-user accounts and case sharing** so adjusters, nurse reviewers, supervisors, and SIU are all looking at the same cited chronology.
- **Word and Excel export** that drops straight into the claim file or the client's reporting template.
- **Volume pricing** on the Insurance & Legal plan; individual adjusters can start today at $0.10 per page.

## Handling the files TPAs actually receive

The medical records that reach a TPA are rarely clean. OctopusLM is built for the realistic case:

- **Out-of-order and mid-page splits** — records are re-sequenced by encounter date, not by the order they were scanned. See [what to do when records arrive out of order](../blog/records-out-of-order-mid-page.html).
- **Duplicates** — identified by content, not just header text, so a re-faxed consult does not appear as a second visit. See [why header matching fails](../blog/duplicate-records-header-vs-content-matching.html).
- **Handwritten notes and poor scans** — flagged where the OCR is uncertain rather than silently guessed.
- **Thousands of pages** — long-tail claims are the normal case.

### Can we set up separate workspaces per client program?

Yes. Multi-user team plans support separate cases and sharing rules, so one client's files are not visible to adjusters working another program. Scoping this is part of the Insurance & Legal plan conversation.

### How is OctopusLM priced for a TPA?

Individual users pay $0.10 per page or $250 per month unlimited. TPA programs are priced as a custom Insurance & Legal plan based on adjuster count and file volume, with multi-user accounts, case sharing, and volume rates. Book a 15-minute demo to scope it.

### Does it replace our nurse reviewers or outside medical review vendor?

Usually it sits in front of them. Adjusters get a cited chronology on day one for triage and reserving; nurse reviewers start from a verified timeline instead of a raw PDF; outside review is reserved for the files that genuinely need a clinical opinion.

### What does the output look like?

A narrative summary plus a dated chronology table with columns for date, provider, event, and source page. Both export to Word and Excel. Every line in the chronology is clickable back to the source page in the browser.

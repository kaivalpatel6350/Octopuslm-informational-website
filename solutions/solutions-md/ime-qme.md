## The records review is the unpaid half of the IME

A defensible IME or QME report rests on a complete records review. The referral arrives with 400 to 4,000 pages: ER notes, imaging, physio, specialist consults, prior IMEs, pharmacy records, sometimes years of family-practice notes. Reading it properly takes hours you are not billing for; skimming it puts your opinion at risk on cross-examination.

## What OctopusLM does with the referral file

Upload the PDFs as received. OctopusLM returns:

- **A dated chronology** of every encounter, complaint, finding, diagnosis, medication, imaging result, and functional statement — in order, with a page citation on every line.
- **A narrative summary** covering mechanism, treatment course, current status, and documented pre-existing or intervening conditions.
- **Clickable citations** — open any entry and land on the source page to check the wording yourself.
- **Word and Excel export** so the chronology can go into your report or your working notes.

The examination is still yours. The opinion is still yours. What changes is that you walk into the room already knowing what is in the file and which pages you want to quote.

## FAQ

### Will the chronology hold up if opposing counsel challenges my records review?

That is what the page citations are for. Every line in the chronology points to the page it came from, so you can show exactly where each fact in your report originates. See our post on [what happens when opposing counsel challenges your chronology](../blog/opposing-counsel-challenges-chronology.html).

### Does OctopusLM write any part of the opinion?

No. It summarizes and organizes the record with citations. Causation, impairment, apportionment, and prognosis remain the examiner's work. We think that boundary is both ethically necessary and what makes the output defensible.

### How do I check the AI output before I rely on it?

Use the citations. Open the entries that matter to your opinion and confirm the wording on the source page. Our [verification checklist](../blog/verifying-ai-output-checklist.html) covers what to check and in what order.

### What about handwritten notes and poor scans?

Where the OCR cannot read a page confidently, the output flags it rather than guessing. You will know which pages need your own eyes.

### Is claimant data handled appropriately?

Yes. Records are encrypted in transit and at rest, processed in HIPAA- and PIPEDA-compliant infrastructure, and never used to train models. OctopusLM is built by a Canadian company and handles both Canadian and US files.
